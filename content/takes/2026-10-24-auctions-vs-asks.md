Asking prices are where sellers start. Hammer prices are where buyers finish. The gap between them is one of the most useful numbers in the market.

When sold lots for a model keep landing well under the median dealer ask, expect that model's asks to soften over the following weeks. When they land above it, dealers tend to reprice upward.
