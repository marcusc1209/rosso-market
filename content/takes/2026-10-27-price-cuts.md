A falling median can mean sellers are cutting, or simply that cheaper cars arrived. We only call a model a mover when the same cars repriced the same way.

That rule throws out a lot of noise. A single high mileage car joining the market can drag a thin model's median by several percent in a day without a single seller changing their mind.
