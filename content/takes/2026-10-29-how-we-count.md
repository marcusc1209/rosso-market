The same 488 Spider can sit on a dealer site, AutoTrader, Cars.com and the Ferrari Approved catalogue at once. Here is how we make sure it moves the median exactly once.

Every car is matched by VIN first. Older cars without a modern VIN are matched by chassis number, and anything left is matched on model, year, mileage band, colour and seller. When one car shows two prices, the selling dealer's own page wins, because marketplaces are often a few days behind a price cut.
