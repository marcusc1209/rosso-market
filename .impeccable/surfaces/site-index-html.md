## Scope

Whole site redesign of `site/index.html`: overview, family, model, car, auctions, news, methodology, privacy, header, search, footer. Mode: Operate. Replacement visual world; product truth, copy and functions stay.

## Audience and task

US Ferrari buyers and collectors pricing a model or a specific car. They scan where asks sit against real sales, open a model, then one car and its history. Constraints: photos often missing, thin data in the first weeks, phone use at dealers and auctions, copy without dashes or semicolons.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Asks and sales printed over each other. Every model is two translucent inks on one log price scale, cobalt for the middle half of asking prices, vermilion for the middle half of sale prices, and where they overlap the ink turns violet: the price where the market clears. Refuses the category default of a black luxury hero, giant red capitals and walls of photo cards.

OWN-WORLD: Milan modernist racing print of the 1950s and 60s, Max Huber's Monza posters and the Ferrari yearbooks: flat translucent primaries overprinted on white paper. Neutral paper #f3f3f0 and white sheets, ink #16161a, cobalt ask, vermilion sold, giallo yellow shell band and selection. One family, Archivo, expanded heavy for page titles, normal width tabular figures for data. Hairline ink rules, square corners, no shadows except on overlays.

STORY: The visitor sees at once where every model's asks sit against what sold, picks a model, reads its overprinted chart and listings, opens a car and follows its sellers and price cuts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Yellow header band with the wordmark, Models, Hypercars, Auctions, News and search. Under it a short paper band: one line headline with as of date and the day's counts in a single facts row. Then the market board filling the rest: family tabs, a sort control, and model rows (name, for sale count, median ask, median sold, the overprint strip on a shared log axis), about ten rows visible at 1440 by 900. The board is the primary action: each row opens its model.

FORM: Huber overprint, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list (sale paper market pages, auction catalogue, Classiche red book, Huber Monza overprint, paint chip cards, timing classification sheets, Touring Club maps). seed:786e19d9c213cc02. Signature move: the overprint strip, reused on the model header, auction cards (estimate against result) and the car page (this car's price ticked against both inks). Motion: inks print in once on first render, 220 ms, colour wipe left to right.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
